# Full Project Preset

Use this preset for long-lived projects, public or shared releases,
multi-person work, projects people rely on, operational needs, security
expectations, or formal releases.

## Use

- `core/README.template.md`
- `core/PROJECT_BRIEF.template.md`
- `core/PLAN.template.md`
- `core/PROJECT_GUIDE.template.md`
- `core/DEVELOPMENT.template.md`
- `optional/TECHNOLOGY.template.md`
- `optional/SECURITY.template.md`
- `optional/DECISION_LOG.template.md`
- `optional/CHANGELOG.template.md`

## Add When Relevant

- `optional/STORAGE.template.md`
- `optional/ROADMAP.template.md`
- `optional/OPERATIONS.template.md`
- `legal/PRIVACY.template.md`
- `legal/TERMS.template.md`
- `legal/DISCLAIMER.template.md`
- `legal/NOTICE.template.md`

## Notes

Use dedicated documents when the details are important enough to maintain over
time. Keep the plan focused on implementation, and move durable choices to the
decision log.
